Likely questions

Why Airudi?
Build a shared platform that product teams depend on, in a scaling company; AI agents in production need guardrails, permissions and traceability. Énergir ended Sept 30; available now.
7+ years, 2+ lead?
Nearly 18 years. No formal lead title: say so. You initiated the shared Jenkins pipelines, were the primary developer contact when things broke, and onboarded new DevOps engineers and developers.
Led a capability used by several teams?
Yes: shared Jenkins templates at Énergir, 12 teams and 70+ apps, mandatory quality checks (SonarQube) developers can't skip. Not every app used them.
Leading without authority?
About 6 enablement sessions a year with each team; failures made self-diagnosing; Salesforce rebuild with no prior experience.
SLOs, error budgets?
Not implemented. You built groundwork (Salesforce logging into Dynatrace, DML error parsing, dashboards); RCA on critical incidents.
Progressive delivery, rollbacks?
MuleSoft: prepare and QA ahead, select by version, rollback redeploys a previous artifact. Build once, deploy many. No canary or blue/green in production.
Kubernetes, Terraform, OpenTelemetry?
Kubernetes application-level, ArgoCD at Desjardins; no cluster ownership. Terraform as a user on Azure. No OpenTelemetry; your tools are Dynatrace, Splunk, ELK.
Multi-tenant SaaS, distributed systems?
The biggest gap. Say you haven't designed multi-tenant architecture; you bring delivery, reliability and tooling.
Policy-as-code, LLM gateway, MCP?
Not built. Adjacent: mandatory pipeline gates, Oracle releases without emergency-access approval, Tink inventory traceability (CVEs, Jira refs from Git logs); AI-assisted practices at Énergir with scope gates and human validation.
Security, SOC 2?
