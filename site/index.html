Selected work

Real problems. Working solutions.

Examples of the systems I’ve delivered in energy and financial services.

Énergir · Delivery standards

Shared pipelines, tested on every commit

The problem
Teams maintained multiple Jenkins pipelines doing the same work, making changes and troubleshooting harder.
What I built
A shared pipeline repository with per-commit unit tests, approximately 80–90% JaCoCo coverage, and sample applications for manual integration testing.
What changed
All teams already deploying through Jenkins adopted the shared pipelines, with clearer reporting to distinguish pipeline defects from application issues.

Énergir · Release automation

A 15-step checklist, automated

The problem
MuleSoft production releases depended on a 15-step manual checklist.
What I built
Jenkins automation built each release once, allowing QA to test the exact artifact that would later be deployed to production.
What changed
Release preparation moved ahead of the production window. Developers selected a tested version to deploy and could roll back by redeploying a previous artifact.

Desjardins · Platform health

Validation across 250 repositories

The problem
The platform team needed to verify both Artifactory configuration and access to upstream artifacts, including through firewall restrictions.
What I built
A Python/FastAPI workflow that provisioned Artifactory, applied configuration from a selected Git reference, and tested artifact downloads without using cached copies.
What changed
Deployed through ArgoCD/GitOps, the suite ran on a schedule and reported configuration versions and artifact-level results in Dynatrace, making failed retrievals visible.

Énergir · Production observability

More insight from Salesforce logs

The problem
Salesforce logs passed through Logstash, while useful error details were not exposed in the existing dashboards.
What I built
Direct Dynatrace ingestion with expanded parsing of DML error types, messages, and originating classes, plus dashboards to explore those details.
What changed
The Salesforce team could investigate previously hidden issues. The solution ran in production for several weeks before handoff to a long-standing team member, establishing groundwork for future SLOs.

My approach

How I work

Start with what hurts.

“If you had a magic wand and could fix one thing, what would you fix first?” That’s often my opening question. Together, we identify who each issue affects, how often it happens, and its impact. That gives us a practical starting point.

Solve the immediate problem.

I investigate the workflow and work with the team to implement a solution that fits their environment. The technology stack shapes the implementation; the problem guides the decisions.

Find more value in what’s already there.

Once the immediate pain is addressed, I introduce practices and capabilities the team may not have explored. Often, their existing tools can do more than they realize.

For example: centralized logs can support much more than troubleshooting after an incident. They can reveal recurring errors, gradual degradation, and early warning signs—even when nothing has caused a major outage. I help teams turn that information into useful monitoring and earlier action.
