SRE terms

  • SLI what you measure · SLO what you aim for · SLA what you promise
  • Error budget = 100% − SLO (99.9% ≈ 43 min / 30 days)
  • Burn rate = how fast the budget is spent
  • Page on fast burns, ticket on slow ones
  • Toil = manual, repetitive operational work
  • Mitigate first, root cause after
  • Freshness · completeness · availability
  • Nothing should fail silently
