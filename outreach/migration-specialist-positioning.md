## The idea

Niche as "the migration guy" rather than generalist DevOps: vSphere → Cloud, Cloud →
Cloud, pipeline-to-pipeline (e.g. Jenkins → GitHub Actions/Azure DevOps). Evergreen
demand — orgs are always moving to "greener grass" — and easier to sell than open-ended
DevOps services because the value proposition is concrete and comparable (migrate X to Y
by date Z), not an ongoing retainer someone has to justify every quarter.

## Why it fits the fixed-price/value-based model ([[firm-comparison]] category 5)

Migrations have a defined start state and end state, so "done" is unambiguous — one of
the few consulting products genuinely suited to outcome pricing instead of hourly
billing. That's structurally different from ongoing DevOps support, where scope drifts
forever and fixed-price is a bad bet.

**Real risk: discovery.** You don't know how gnarly a migration is until you're inside
it (undocumented dependencies, data quality issues, hardcoded assumptions). Quoting flat
fixed-price before discovery risks eating every surprise. The standard playbook: **two
phases** —
1. Paid discovery/assessment engagement (hourly or small fixed fee) — map actual scope,
   dependencies, risk.
2. Fixed-price execution phase, priced off what discovery found.

Never skip straight to fixed-price off a sales call before looking under the hood.
