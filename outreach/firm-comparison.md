# Engagement Model Taxonomy & Firm Comparison

Framework for classifying staffing agencies / consulting firms by how they actually pay
and manage you, independent of how they market themselves. Useful for screening any new
firm (see [[levio-call-notes]]) and for scoring postings against profile.md.

## The models

1. **Full-time employee (FTE)** — salary, benefits, paid vacation/sick/holidays. Not
   using the incorporated company. Not what Marc is targeting (see profile.md).

2. **Pure independent contractor (own corp)** — hourly, bill-what-you-work via the
   corporation. No pay if sick, no paid holidays, no benefits from the placing firm.
   Marc bills his rate + sales tax; the firm adds its margin and bills the client.
   **Cofina** is this model — a few check-in calls a year (3-4x), pure T&M pass-through.

3. **Consulting-agency employee (CGI / Accenture / Deloitte model)** — salaried employee
   of the agency (T4, not billing through your corp), full benefits, paid vacation, and
   critically **bench pay** — paid between client assignments while they staff you on
   the next one.

4. **Agency-as-employer-of-record, no real bench pay** — the trap case. On paper you're
   an "employee" (T4, statutory minimums), sold in interviews as model #3, but
   functionally identical to #2: paid only for hours worked, let go (not benched) when
   the contract ends. Distinguishing question: *"If I'm between client assignments, do
   I keep getting paid, or is that a layoff?"* #3 says yes without hesitation; #4 hedges.

   Note: check-in **frequency** is a separate axis from payment model — it signals
   management style/autonomy (low-touch vs. micromanagement), not whether you get paid
   between contracts. Don't conflate a low-touch check-in cadence with confirmation of
   bench pay; ask the bench-pay question directly regardless of how the check-ins feel.

(There's also a long-term goal, category 5: fixed-price/milestone value-based consulting
— the "Million Dollar Consulting" model, priced on outcome not hours. Not a near-term
option; requires brand/referral network Marc doesn't have yet. Build toward it via case
studies from #2/#3 engagements, not instead of them.)
