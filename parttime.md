# Fractional DevOps / Platform Consulting Plan

## Purpose

Add a second job-search track to the existing career/resume system focused specifically on **part-time / fractional senior DevOps and Platform Engineering consulting engagements**.

This is separate from the primary search for Senior/Staff Platform Engineer, DevOps, SRE, or infrastructure leadership positions.

The objective is not to find a traditional second full-time job.

The target is a small, high-value consulting engagement that can run alongside the primary consulting role.

---

# Target Engagement

Ideal engagement:

* Fractional Senior Platform / DevOps Engineer
* A manageable part-time capacity, initially expected to be approximately **8–15 hours per week**
* Fully remote
* Preferably long-term: **6–12+ months**
* Primarily asynchronous
* Limited meetings
* No routine 24/7 on-call responsibilities
* B2B/contract engagement where possible
* **$90+ CAD/hour** when an hourly arrangement is necessary
* Preferred end state: a **monthly retainer with no hours attached**, priced for defined access,
  responsibility, and outcomes rather than time
* Canada is easiest administratively, but remote US/international consulting opportunities should also be considered where practical

The goal is to add approximately **$40K–$70K+ in annual consulting revenue** without taking on another full-time workload.

---

# Positioning

Do **not** primarily market this as:

> Senior DevOps Engineer looking for part-time work.

Instead, position it around the business problem being solved:

> **Fractional Senior Platform / DevOps Engineer**
>
> Helping engineering teams improve cloud infrastructure, deployment systems, automation, and platform reliability without hiring another full-time senior engineer.

The value proposition is ongoing access to senior expertise and ownership of defined problems
without hiring another employee. Do not sell the retainer as a block of spare engineering hours.

Potential buyers include:

* CTOs
* VP Engineering
* Heads of Engineering
* Platform Engineering leaders
* Startups
* Scale-ups
* Small/mid-sized engineering organizations
* Companies with developers currently maintaining infrastructure themselves
* Teams with DevOps/platform technical debt
* Organizations that need senior infrastructure expertise but cannot justify another senior FTE

# Opportunity Evaluation

When a potential contract is found, evaluate it against the following criteria.

### Strong Fit

* Manageable alongside the primary role; roughly 5–15 hours/week if explicitly hourly
* $90+ CAD/hour for hourly work, or a worthwhile monthly retainer with no hours attached
* Senior-level responsibility
* Infrastructure/platform ownership
* Terraform/IaC
* Kubernetes/cloud
* CI/CD
* Reliability/automation
* Architecture/advisory component
* Remote
* Flexible schedule
* Async-friendly
* Limited meetings
* No routine on-call

### Possible Fit

* 15–20 hours/week
* Short-term project
* Lower initial rate but strategically valuable
* Fixed-scope assessment/project
* Significant technical challenge
* Strong potential for recurring work

### Likely Reject

* Effectively a second full-time job
* Daily meeting-heavy schedule
* Routine 24/7 on-call
* Junior/mid-level execution work
* Low hourly rate
* Staff augmentation requiring constant availability
* Fixed 9–5 working window
* Roles requiring frequent travel/office attendance

# Application Workflow

For every interesting opportunity:

1. Capture the complete job/project description.
2. Compare it against `profile.md`.
3. Determine actual technical and seniority fit.
4. Identify missing requirements.
5. Flag deal-breakers such as hours, on-call, location, rate, or schedule.
6. Estimate whether the opportunity is worth pursuing.
7. Extract the most relevant verified accomplishments.
8. Generate a targeted pitch.
9. Generate a tailored CV/profile if necessary.
10. Prepare interview/discovery-call talking points.

Do not blindly apply based on keyword overlap.

The objective is **high-quality, selective applications**.

---

# Rate Strategy

Transitional hourly floor:

**$90+ CAD/hour**

Preferred end state:

**A monthly retainer with no hours attached.** The client buys defined access, responsibility,
continuity, and outcomes—not a prepaid bucket of engineering time.

Rate should depend on:

* Engagement duration
* Weekly commitment
* Technical complexity
* Advisory vs implementation work
* On-call expectations
* Meeting requirements
* Client location
* Contract structure
* Whether the work is fixed-scope or hourly

Do not automatically lower the rate simply because the engagement is part-time. Do not derive a
retainer by multiplying a promised number of hours by an hourly rate.

A fractional client is buying access to senior expertise and ongoing ownership without paying the
cost of another full-time senior employee.

---

# Revenue Model

Hourly work is an acceptable bridge at **$90+ CAD/hour**, but it is not the destination.

The target is recurring monthly revenue from one or more clearly bounded retainers. Each retainer
must define the problems covered, access and response expectations, communication cadence,
exclusions, and how out-of-scope work is approved. Capacity is monitored internally, but no number
of hours is sold or attached to the client agreement.

The preferred outcome is **high-value recurring revenue for sustained responsibility and
outcomes**, with a workload that remains manageable alongside the primary role.
